# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project is vendor-neutral and works on contested ground (interoperability between competing
implementations). Disagreement about *technical* behaviour is the point of the project; it should never become
personal.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique code, evidence and claims, not people.
- Report what you observed, with versions and files, and say what you did *not* verify.
- Give credit, and accept credit for corrections.
- Treat contributor-provided documents and data with care; never attempt to re-identify anyone.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information, including un-anonymized documents, without permission.
- Pressuring maintainers or other implementers' communities to change behaviour in bad faith, or misrepresenting
  results to favour or disparage a product.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to public spaces when
someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of
Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions follow [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

To report a problem, use GitHub's private route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or contact a maintainer
directly through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
